Artists & Genres

The sounds I play in my DJ sets and the ones I travel to see live.

My hype meter

How hyped I get when each genre comes on, on a scale of 0 to 10.

House / Tech House

Around 120 to 128 BPM

Groovy, bouncy, and built for dancing all night. This is what I play most when I DJ. Artists: John Summit, Chris Lake, Fisher, Dom Dolla.

10 out of 10 10 / 10

Dubstep / Bass

Around 140 to 150 BPM

Heavy drops and wobbles that shake the whole field. Perfect for headbanging. Artists: Subtronics, Excision, Zeds Dead, Svdden Death.

9 out of 10 9 / 10

Techno

Around 125 to 140 BPM

Dark, driving, and hypnotic. Better in a warehouse at 2 AM than in the daytime. Artists: Charlotte de Witte, Amelie Lens, Adam Beyer.

7 out of 10 7 / 10

Drum & Bass

Around 170 to 175 BPM

Fast breakbeats and rolling basslines. The energy never lets up. Artists: Sub Focus, Chase & Status, Netsky.

6 out of 10 6 / 10

Top 5 artists to see live

  1. Dom Dolla – every set feels like a party with friends.
  2. Excision – the loudest, heaviest production I've ever felt.
  3. Fisher – pure fun, the whole crowd sings along to "Losing It."
  4. Chris Lake – perfect tech house, always smooth transitions.
  5. John Summit – the set that made me want to start DJing.
